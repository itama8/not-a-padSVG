A supercharged notepad: plain text and a tiny UI. Everything else lives in this x-ray layer, which appears when you hesitate. Hold to peek, then follow a path to an action; tap to keep it on. On your phone there is no system keyboard: the app draws its own input surface. Open source on desktop. These tasks are coming:

Hold anywhere to show the x-ray, then follow a path to a tile. Tap to keep it on. On a keyboard, hold Alt, or press Enter on the wordmark.
