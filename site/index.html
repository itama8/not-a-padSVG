A supercharged notepad: plain text and a tiny UI. Everything else lives in this x-ray layer, which appears when you hesitate. Hold to peek, then follow a path to an action; tap to keep it on. On a desktop, hold or tap Alt. On your phone there is no system keyboard: the app draws its own input surface. Open source on desktop: star it on GitHub. Each reveal shows a few of the tasks to come: lists, calendar and meetings, daily notes, AI agents, meal plans, wedding plans, workouts, technical explanations, random stuff, budgeting, journaling, travel and vacation planning, date planning, pets, family, a calculator, recipes, a lab book, inventory, a phonebook and RSS. Any AI can plug in: OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Meta Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek, Grok, Qwen, Hugging Face, OpenRouter, GitHub Copilot and Codex work today; Perplexity, Cursor, Claude Code, Ollama and your own AI are coming.
