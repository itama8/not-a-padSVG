Keyboard integration

Direct keyboard · Scatter lab

Tests: the phone keyboard inside the real home screen. Try: open the keyboard tile in X-Ray, type two lines with Enter, and tap Send. Paste in the clipboard check to verify both lines. Tap the keyboard tile again or × to close and keep a draft. Switch preview sizes while open: controls should use clear space beside the keyboard, and the wordmark should move only when it overlaps.
