Tile lab: four tiles, new concepts

Each tile plays on a loop, big (2x) and at real phone size (54px). Blue is what you write or own, graphite is the app. Tap any tile to replay it. Pick a letter per tile, or say what idea you meant.

Budgeting

Now

A ring chart with a blue share, and a coin stack.

A · Tally

You jot a spend; it rules the line and the total grows to match.

B · Jars

Money goes into named jars; a coin drops in and that jar fills.

C · Left to spend

You log a spend; the bar fills and it measures what's left before your limit.

AI agents (the hub the AI badges plug into)

Now

A network graph: a blue dot comes in, edges flow out.

A · Ask, it answers

You write a request; the agent writes the answer under it, line by line.

B · Hand it off

You write one task; the agent splits it into steps and ticks them off.

C · Switchboard

Your request goes in; any AI can plug into a pin, and they light up in turn.

Inventory

Now

Stacked boxes with a blue label; the top box lands.

A · Count

Take one off the shelf and your count goes down with it.

B · Find it

You wrote where it went; it checks each drawer and finds the one you labelled.

C · Running low

When the jar drops below your line, it goes onto the shopping list.

Pets

Now

A paw print with a blue tag; the toes pulse.

A · Fed today

Food in the bowl, and today gets its tick, so nobody feeds them twice.

B · Walks

Each walk, step by step along the route you planned.

C · Their tag

Everything about them on one tag: their name in your hand, and it swings.
