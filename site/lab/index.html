Labs

Small experiments, one interaction at a time. Each lab names what it tests, what to try, and what good looks like — so a visitor can judge the idea in under a minute without reading the source.

  • Counter routingTap the total, then send a scripted burst. Tests: real server detection diverts clicks into a stored red total. Good: reload retains the total and the flag.
  • SVG museum
  • Tile artworkCompares budgeting, agents, inventory, and pets artwork at 108px and real 54px phone size. Tests: reads at both sizes, motion replays, currency sign. Good: the idea survives 54px.
  • TouchprintsMarks every contact point with a fingerprint stamp for demos. Tests: the print reads at 44–84px, stays distinct from app icons, and clears without hiding the surface. Good: the touch point is unmistakable and the layer never blocks a control.
  • Desktop scatterExplores X-Ray tile layout: mood, relaxation, density, balance. Tests: 14 tiles + 8 badges fit a 1280×800 stage without covering the wordmark. Good: balanced, clear of blockers, reproducible by seed.
  • Keyboard integrationThe phone keyboard inside the real home screen. Tests: find the keyboard tile, type a line, Return copies it. Good: typing, motion, and clipboard all work at desktop and phone widths.
  • Direct keyboardThe keyboard alone, no home screen. Tests: open, press, spring release, preview bubble. Good: motion timing and the launcher icon in isolation.
