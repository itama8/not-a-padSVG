Every sheet shows the home page's real marks at real sizes, with the x-ray on. Blue is always your ink. Graphite is the app's ink, so on dark paper it turns to chalk. Faint marks are lifted on dark paper so the x-ray keeps its hierarchy.

Notepad and Slate are the current defaults for a light and a dark device. Open "try it full screen" to hold and tap on the real page.
